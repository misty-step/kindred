Kindred prototypes

Static prototypes with a simulated room. Bots stand in for friends and an authored synonym list stands in for the judge. Rationale: design/reimagine/README.md. Spec for the recommended direction: design/reimagine/DESIGN.md.

Kindred (recommended)

One rule, one screen per moment, color only for matches. Answers flip, then matching ones gather into a colored group.

Pairs (round 5, current direction)

When exactly two of you say the same thing, you both score. Three or more, nobody does. The pair with the most points wins. Nothing is seeded. See MECHANICS.md, round 5.

Constellation (alternate)

Meld (alternate, two players)

If you miss, both answers become the next clue, and you try to meet in the middle. Fewer tries is better.
